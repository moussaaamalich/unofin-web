Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last Updated: November 2026 • Developer: Moussa Aamalich • Platform: Windows Desktop (Microsoft Store)
Zero-Knowledge & Cloudless Privacy Guarantee: UnoFin™ for Windows is architected as a privacy-first personal finance application with 100% local SQLite ledger storage, requiring internet connectivity solely for optional sponsored partner banners and update checks.

1. 100% Local Storage & Zero-Knowledge Architecture

  • Local Sandboxing: All personal financial data—including account balances, income streams, 50/30/20 budgets, debt payoff schedules, transactions, savings checkpoints, and net worth entries—resides exclusively on your Windows device inside an isolated local SQLite database (unofin.db).
  • No Mandatory Cloud Servers: UnoFin™ operates zero external financial storage servers and requires no account registration, email address, username, or phone number.
  • Zero Data Commercialization: Your private financial figures are never accessed, monitored, collected, transmitted, or sold.

2. Advertising & Partner Recommendations Disclosure

  • Ad & Partner-Supported Free Edition: UnoFin™ for Windows is provided free of charge and may display non-intrusive display advertisements or curated recommendations for trusted third-party financial tools.
  • Affiliate Disclosure: Some partner links or banners displayed within the desktop interface may be affiliate referrals. If you choose to visit or sign up with a recommended service, the developer may receive a referral commission at no additional cost to you.
  • Strict Data Isolation: Third-party ad networks or affiliate landing pages are completely isolated from your local SQLite database and have zero access to your balances, budgets, or ledger entries.

3. Uno AI Copilot & Ephemeral On-Device Simulations

  • On-Device Processing: Uno AI runs locally on your machine. Your financial prompts and simulation queries are never sent to external cloud AI servers or third-party data brokers.
  • Ephemeral Sessions: Conversational interactions with Uno AI are transient and clear automatically when navigating away or closing the session.

4. Local Security & Access Protection

  • Local Lock Protection: Optional PIN or OS-level security verification (such as Windows Hello where supported) is handled locally on your device. UnoFin™ never stores or transmits raw biometric signatures.

5. Financial, Investment & Tax Disclaimer

  • Educational Bookkeeping Only: UnoFin™ (including 50/30/20 Studio, Debt Snowball vs. Avalanche, Net Worth analytics, and Cashflow Radar) is provided strictly for personal bookkeeping and educational simulation.
  • Zero Bank Connectivity: UnoFin™ contains no open-banking APIs, does not connect to banks or payment processors, and never executes real-world financial transfers or debits.
  • Not Certified Financial Advice: Always consult a licensed financial advisor, accountant, or legal professional before making major financial commitments.

6. User Data Custody & Backups

  • Sole Custody: Because your database is stored locally on your PC, uninstalling the application or deleting local app data will erase your ledger unless you maintain backups. Users are encouraged to regularly export local JSON backups, Excel spreadsheets, and PDF statements using the built-in export tools.

7. Children's Privacy & Limitation of Liability

UnoFin™ is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). The software is provided "AS IS" without warranties of any kind; the developer disclaims liability for calculation discrepancies or device-level data loss.
